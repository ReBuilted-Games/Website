Privacy Policy

When users sign in with Roblox, this site displays only public profile information needed for the experience.

What we access

  • Your Roblox display name and username.
  • Your public avatar/profile image.

What we do not collect

We do not store your password or private Roblox credentials. The site only uses the information required to display your profile while signed in.
