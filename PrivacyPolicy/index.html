Privacy Policy

This site only uses Roblox public profile information for the sign-in experience. It does not collect passwords or private account credentials.

What we access

  • Roblox display name and username.

What we do not collect

We do not store your Roblox password or any secure authentication tokens in the browser. Only public profile fields are shown while connected.
